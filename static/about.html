About the festival

A national-level celebration of culture, creativity, sport and student talent.

What is COLORIDO?

COLORIDO 2K26 is a college-level cultural and sports event designed to bring students together for competition, creativity and collaboration. The platform gives participants one place to discover events, read rules and schedules, register online, and check festival updates.
